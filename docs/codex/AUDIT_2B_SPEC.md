THE RULES (put them in ONE shared module, src/services/accountingBasis.js, with pure helpers in src/utils/accountingBasis.js, and make every screen below use it — no screen computes these on its own):
R1 Revenue for a period = delivered value: sum of v_delivered_revenue.line_value with waybill_date in the period (one row per waybill; price = that order's average price for the block type). Rows with unvalued = true are listed as "Delivered but unpriced" and excluded from the total. Cash received is a separate figure called "Cash received" = confirmed payments (payments.status = 'confirmed') by payment_date. Never call cash "revenue".
R2 Customer position as at date D, per customer: delivered value up to D (waybills joined to orders.customer_id, waybill_date <= D) minus confirmed payments up to D (payments → invoices → orders.customer_id, payment_date <= D). Positive = Trade receivable. Negative = Customer deposit (a liability: money received for blocks not yet delivered). Totals: Trade receivables = sum of positives; Customer deposits = sum of |negatives|. Drafts/proformas and cancelled invoices never count. Invoices remain the collection document: AR aging still ages issued/paid invoices, but its grand total must reconcile to Trade receivables + note any difference ("Delivered but not yet invoiced" / "Invoiced but not yet delivered").
R3 Expenses in profit = expenses with status/approval_status 'approved' only, by expense_date. Pending expenses are shown under the total as "Awaiting approval: ₦X (not included)". Rejected never appear in totals.
R4 Cost of blocks: finished-goods stock on the balance sheet is valued at production cost per block = the same calculation the Production Cost Analysis report uses (Reports.jsx 'production_cost'): materials + labour for the period ÷ blocks produced, per block type, using the latest 3 months that have production; if no cost is available for a block type, show the quantity and "not valued" and leave it out of the total. NEVER value stock at selling price.
R5 Payables = supplier ledger: per supplier, purchases − payments − returns (src/utils/supplierBalance.js) up to D. Same figure on every screen.
R6 Site scope: when a site is selected, revenue (waybills/handovers of that site), cash (payments on orders of that site), and costs (expenses with that site_id) are ALL that site's; the company view is all sites. Never mix company-wide cash with site-only costs.
R7 "As at" dates: every balance-sheet figure uses only records dated on or before D: bank = opening balance + transactions to D; stock as at D = current balance minus movements dated after D (materials: stock_movements after D reversed; blocks: finished_goods_stock now minus batches accepted after D plus waybill quantity_loaded after D plus batch damage after D); receivables/deposits per R2; payables per R5. If a figure cannot be computed historically, show it with "current figure — history not available" instead of silently showing today's number under an old date.
R8 Adjustments (financial_adjustments: statement_type, account_name, amount, period_from, period_to, adjustment_date): an adjustment affects only its own statement_type and only when its adjustment_date (or period_from–period_to) falls in the selected period. Income-statement adjustments always flow into net profit (positive = income, negative = cost) — not only when the name contains "tax". Balance-sheet adjustments are included in the totals they are listed under. Cash-flow adjustments are period-filtered and counted once, under "Other adjustments". (There are 0 adjustments today; build it right before they are used.)
R9 BDM sees no profit, cost or margin figures (expenses RLS excludes BDM): hide those views/cards for the BDM role (Reports P&L/Balance sheet/Cash flow/Expense are already role-limited — check KPI Dashboard and the main Dashboard cards); do not show partial figures.
